# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful
- Be constructive
- Be professional
- Be collaborative

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Abusive language
- Trolling

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove content or contributors that violate this Code of Conduct.
